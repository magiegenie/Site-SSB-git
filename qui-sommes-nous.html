Paying made effortless with
the devices you carry daily

iPhone

Open Apple Wallet, tap the plus sign, then choose Jeton Card and confirm to finish setup.

Android

Open Google Pay, tap your profile, then Payment methods and Add payment method to add your Jeton Card.

Apple Watch

Open Apple Wallet on your watch, tap Add Card and pick Jeton Card to get started.

Wear OS

Open Google Pay on your watch, tap Get started, set a screen lock if needed, then add your card from your phone.

iPad & Mac

Open System Settings and select Wallet & Apple Pay to add your Jeton Card in a few taps.

Tablet & Computer

Open Payment methods, choose Add payment method, fill in your card details, then save and confirm.
